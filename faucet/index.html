How it works

  1. An earthquake happens. Every magnitude 5+ quake is published on the blockchain a few minutes later. Its magnitude is the median of three seismology catalogs: USGS, EMSC and GEOFON.
  2. It pays 1,000 MW5 per point above 5. M5.7 pays 700, M6.7 pays 1,700, M7.8 pays 2,800. There is no maximum.
  3. The pot adds up. Three quakes of M5.3, M5.4 and M5.3 make 0.3 + 0.4 + 0.3 = 1 point: 1,000 MW5.
  4. Up to 3 quakes. Your pot keeps the last 3 quakes you have not claimed. If a 4th quake arrives and you still have not claimed, the pot empties and starts again from the next quake. So come back often.
  5. Claim with your address. Paste it and press Claim: the MW5 arrive in your wallet and the network fee is paid for you. No wallet to connect, no limit on how much you can collect.
