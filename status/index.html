Fifty years of a restless planet

30-day M5+ count MW5 level (v2)

USGS catalog since 1973, rescaled to the feed value of today, peak of every 15 days. The MW5 level is simulated day by day with the v2 rules: 90-day average, soft curve, at most 0.7% a day. Below ×1 the pool burns and the price goes up; above ×1 holders receive new coins. Holder balances never go down. This series uses 15-day peaks, so it runs a little hotter than the daily feed. A simulation, not a promise.
