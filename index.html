Balances follow e−(Q² − 189²) / 330², with Q capped at 400. Moving from one count to another multiplies your coins by the ratio of the two curve values. On a mint you receive 99.3% of it; on a burn you lose the burn plus 0.7%. The price is assumed not to move, so the dollar value changes only with your coin count. A change of more than 150 earthquakes is applied over several updates. An estimate, not financial advice.

Two shares. Nothing for the team.

At launch the contract replays a "historical burn": from zero earthquakes to the real value of the feed on launch day. With 189 quakes that burn is 27.96% of the supply. Those coins are not destroyed: they go to the community, as airdrops and a faucet.

How it works

From the seismometer to your wallet.

  1. 01

    Three catalogs count

    USGS, EMSC and GEOFON each count the magnitude 5+ earthquakes of the last 30 days. Every Opsikom node takes the median of the three.

  2. 02

    The oracle publishes

    Independent nodes sign the value. The Opsikom contract checks the quorum and publishes the median of the nodes, every 3 days.

  3. 03

    Anyone calls update()

    Whoever calls update() on MW5 applies the new value and earns a bounty: 0.1% of the coins burned or minted, with no cap.

  4. 04

    Wallets breathe

    Every holder's balance is multiplied by the same factor. More quakes: burn. Fewer quakes: mint. Even a change of one earthquake counts.

Only holders move. The liquidity pools (V2 and V3) and the airdrop & faucet wallet are excluded: their balances never burn or mint. The price stays the one of the pools, so the value of a wallet really goes up and down with the Earth.
Fees

0.7%, and all of it goes to liquidity.

There is no team wallet and no tax for anybody. Every fee is collected by the contract and added to the V2 pool, with the LP tokens burned.

Why sells are free. A pool expects to receive exactly the amount it is sold. A fee on sells would make V2 swaps fail and V3 refuse the payment: MW5 could not be sold at all.
